<p class="profile-kicker">RELIABLE &amp; EFFICIENT AI</p>
<p class="profile-lead">I study how AI systems can reason with incomplete evidence, act reliably over long horizons, and use computation efficiently.</p>
<div><h2 style="margin-top:15px">About</h2></div><div><p>I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from McMaster University in June 2025, supervised by Professor Jun Chen. My research spans structure-grounded language-model reasoning, agent evaluation, computer vision, image restoration, and federated learning.</p><p>I was a Visiting Researcher at New York University’s Tandon School of Engineering from January to May 2024, working with Professor Chen Feng on computer vision and 3D perception. My work includes the design and evaluation of long-horizon multi-agent systems, efficient language-model adaptation and quantization, RGB-D perception, and vision systems for intelligent transportation. I connect research questions with algorithms, controlled experiments, and prototype systems.</p><p>I am interested in research collaborations and opportunities across academia and industry, including postdoctoral and faculty roles.</p></div>
<h2>Research interests</h2>
<ul><li>Structure-grounded language-model reasoning and active evidence acquisition</li><li>Reliable long-horizon agents and evidence-based evaluation</li><li>Computer vision, image restoration, and 3D perception</li><li>Efficient language models and distributed learning</li></ul>
